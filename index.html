Vision Overview

Replacing screenshot clout and reckless PnL with transparent, risk-adjusted proof of skill on Solana.

01

What is OlympiFI

OlympiFI is a Solana-native gamified social trading platform that combines Instagram-style profiles, skill-based progression, private trading sandboxes, competitions, and progressive feature unlocks.

Instead of relying on unverified claims, followers discover authentic traders whose ranking reflects verifiable execution history and risk discipline.

02

Core Idea

Trading becomes social and aspirational. Traders level up through proven results rather than social media noise or survivorship bias.

Higher ranks unlock the ability to host competitions, run paid live trade streams, and reveal deeper layers of their proprietary strategy to an engaged audience.

03

Key Platform Features

Designed as a comprehensive trading ecosystem engineered for both elite practitioners and emerging market learners:

  • Public Trader Profiles Followable identities showcasing verified equity curves, win rates, and earned badges.
  • Skill-Based Leveling Progression calculated via risk-adjusted returns, max drawdown resistance, and consistency.
  • Private Sandbox Trades stay shielded by default, allowing users to build verified proof without front-running or social pressure.
  • Progressive Visibility 3-stage revealing mechanism: aggregate PnL % → token & direction → complete position sizing.
  • Chat & Community Layer High-signal event feeds, trade theses, and discussions anchored directly to on-chain orders.
  • Competitions & Live Streams High-ranking operators unlock the ability to host mini-tournaments and broadcast paid live trading sessions.
  • Clans & Team Leagues Cooperative trading syndicates competing in weekly seasonal leagues for shared leaderboard glory.
  • Solana-Native Engine Official Solana Foundation token registry, Token-2022 extensions, Jupiter swap routing, and gasless UX.

04

Why It Matters

Most social trading apps force traders to show everything publicly from day one, exposing alpha to front-running and creating unbearable social performance pressure.

OlympiFI gives traders a private sandbox to develop mastery in stealth, then gradually unlocks visibility, status, and monetization tools as they prove themselves on-chain. This establishes an authentic, transparent hierarchy of skill.
